MultiCalc provides calculation tools. Numbers and other values you enter to perform calculations are intended to be processed for the requested calculations. See the app's settings for any available history-management options.

If advertising is enabled in your app version, Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data necessary for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation, subject to their applicable policies and your device settings.

App settings or calculation history, if supported, may be stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app generally removes app-managed local data, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Google and its partners control retention of data processed through their services according to their applicable policies.

For questions about data handling or deletion requests, contact the publisher using the details below.

We seek to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the app and services it integrates. No digital service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

MultiCalc is a general-purpose calculator application. Children may have different privacy protections depending on their age and location. You can manage applicable advertising and privacy preferences through your Android device and Google account settings. You can also contact us about this Privacy Policy or information associated with your use of MultiCalc.
